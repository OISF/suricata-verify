# Description

When the flow memcap is reached, workers free up a flow from the flow table
unless it was seen recently. Flows bypassed with eBPF or XDP must not be taken:
their packets are no longer seen by Suricata, so they always look idle, and ending
them there loses their counters and leaves their entries in the eBPF maps.

50 flows are bypassed. 32 seconds later, new flows exhaust the flow memcap. All
bypassed flows have to be in the flow table until the shutdown.
`flow.get_used_failed` shows that the memcap was reached.
