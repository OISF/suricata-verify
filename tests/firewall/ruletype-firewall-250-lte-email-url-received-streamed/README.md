# ruletype-firewall-250-lte-email-url-received-streamed

Per-buffer validation of the `email.url` and `email.received`
`app_stream` registrations - the streaming multi-buffers of the email MIME
parser (multi-buffer value lists that keep growing while the MIME streams
during SMTP DATA, progress request_data).

## Scenario

Two LTE rules, both with hook `smtp:<request_data`:

* sid:211 `email.url; content:"urlA.example.com"`
* sid:212 `email.received; content:"a.example.com"`

Pcap (generated by `generate-pcap.py`) carries two SMTP sessions. The
first MIME packet carries only the From line; the Received header and the
body (with the URL) arrive in the second packet. On the first DATA pass the
multi-buffers are empty - their no-matches there are PROVISIONAL (the
multi-buffers are streaming: the MIME keeps streaming at their registered
progress) and must not be converted to CANT_MATCH.

* A (port 49290): the second packet carries the Received header and the URL
  "http://urlA.example.com/x"; sid:211 (`email.url`) matches and the flow is
  accepted; sid:212 is not evaluated (first match accepts).
* B (port 49291): no Received header and no URL; no keyword matches; the
  flow is dropped once when the mail completes - the multi-buffer engines'
  own eof CANT_MATCH (the same eof mapping as the single-buffer engines)
  rejects the rules; streaming defers the decision, it never waives the
  drop.

## Expected

exactly 1 `firewall default app policy` drop (flow B).
